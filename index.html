? How To Play

To solve a Sudoku, every square must contain a number from 1 to 9. Each number must appear exactly once in every 3×3 box, in every row, and in every column.

Tools:

Add or remove notes in a square.

Activate it, then select a square to clear all its notes.

Reveals one correct number.

In 2012, the Finnish mathematician Arto Inkala created what is widely considered the hardest solvable Sudoku puzzle in the world. The numerical puzzle took Inkala three months to complete and in a sudoku leveling system where 1 is considered for beginners and 5 takes longer to complete, Inkalas sudoku is reated as an 11 on the difficulty scale.
